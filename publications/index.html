Journal papers

  1. JHEP 2026LHCb Collaboration

    Measurement of the W → μν cross-sections as a function of the muon transverse momentum in pp collisions at √s = 5.02 TeV

    JHEP 03 (2026) 148

    My role: led the analysis and built the full analysis chain as my PhD project. The paper introduces a new way to extract mW from unfolded differential cross-sections.

    • Paper
    • arXiv:2509.18817
    • INSPIRE
  2. Comput. Softw. Big Sci. 2025

    The LHCb Sprucing and Analysis Productions

    A. Abdelmotteleb et al. · Comput. Softw. Big Sci. 9 (2025) 15

    Documents LHCb’s centralised offline processing and analysis-production systems for Run 3. My role: tested and validated the Sprucing, LHCb’s central offline data-reduction stage, ahead of Run 3 data taking.

    • Paper
    • arXiv:2506.20309
    • INSPIRE
  3. JINST 2024LHCb Collaboration

    Curvature-bias corrections using a pseudomass method

    JINST 19 (2024) P03010

    A data-driven correction of momentum-scale biases, a key ingredient for precision electroweak measurements such as mW. My role: designed and implemented the calibration and data-processing code in C++.

    • Paper
    • arXiv:2311.04670
    • INSPIRE
  4. Eur. Phys. J. C 2022

    A new maximum-likelihood method for template fits

    H. Dembinski & A. Abdelmotteleb · Eur. Phys. J. C 82 (2022) 1043

    A fast approximate likelihood for fitting simulated templates, including weighted ones, to data, implemented in the iminuit fitting package. My role: verified the derivations, ran the coverage tests and contributed to the Python implementation.

    • Paper
    • arXiv:2206.12346
    • Code: iminuit.cost.Template
    • INSPIRE
