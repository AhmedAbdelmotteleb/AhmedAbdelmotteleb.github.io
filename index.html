Precision W boson mass

The W mass is one of the Standard Model’s most sensitive tests for new physics. I extract it from the shape of the muon momentum spectrum, using a reusable, detector-corrected measurement that other experiments are now adopting.

About

From Cairo to CERN

I grew up in Cairo, studied physics at the University of Manchester (MPhys, with a thesis on heavy Majorana neutrinos), and did my PhD at the University of Warwick on the W boson at LHCb, where I am now a postdoctoral research fellow. Along the way I spent six months on data quality for the STAR experiment at Brookhaven National Laboratory.

I care about teaching and good tooling: I have taught Python, Git and quantum computing to more than a hundred early-career physicists through LHCb Starterkit and CERN schools, and much of my work ends up as software other people use.

Outside the lab: former national sprint champion, lifelong Manchester United fan, drummer, swing dancer, skydiver, and fantasy and sci-fi reader. I have also visited 26 countries and counting.
