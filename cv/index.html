Experience

  1. Nov 2024 – present

    Postdoctoral Research Fellow, LHCb

    University of Warwick, Department of Physics

    • Lead analyst for the W boson mass measurement with the full Run 2 dataset at 13 TeV, as a double-differential measurement in muon pT and η.
    • Lead analyst for Bc± → Bs(∗)0π± (Run 1+2): ML event selection and zfit mass fit.
    • Leading the 20-person TORCH test-beam analysis team (2022, 2025).
    • Built and maintain the LHCb Analysis Repository Template, now the collaboration’s default analysis skeleton.
    • Physics reviewer for LHCb analyses, including two submitted to Physical Review Letters.
    • Supervisor of Warwick URSS summer projects on normalising flows and quantum computing.
  2. Oct 2020 – Oct 2024

    PhD Researcher, LHCb

    University of Warwick, Department of Physics

    • Led the first LHCb measurement of the forward W → μν differential cross-section in muon pT at 5.02 TeV (JHEP 2026).
    • Developed a modular, reusable route to the W mass via a detector-corrected cross-section, now being adopted by other experiments.
    • Co-developed a new maximum-likelihood method for template fits, implemented in iminuit (EPJC 2022).
    • Contributed to LHCb’s real-time data-processing and trigger software.
  3. Jun 2019 – Jan 2020

    Research Assistant, STAR experiment

    American University in Cairo, with Brookhaven National Laboratory (part-time, alongside my MPhys)

    • Led a team of five young scientists on quark–gluon plasma data-quality studies, presenting results at weekly international meetings.
